How much turf for a circular lawn?

Short answer: A circular lawn 4 m across is 12.6 m². Order about 14 rolls, allowing 10% for cutting the curve.

    Turf for round lawns (+10%)

    Area of a circle = π × radius². Curves waste more turf than straight edges, so allow 10% extra rather than 5%.

    Cutting a neat curve

    Lay the turf over the whole area first, overlapping the edge, then cut the curve. Mark it with a hosepipe or a string and peg from the centre, and cut with a half-moon edger or an old bread knife. It gives a much cleaner line than trying to cut each roll to shape.

    For curved borders, split the lawn into a rectangle plus half-circles, add them up and use the 'I know the area' option in the calculator.

    Questions people ask

    How do I work out the area of an oval lawn?

    Multiply half the length × half the width × 3.14.

    Does the calculator handle circles?

    Yes. Choose 'Circle' and enter the diameter. Note it adds 5%, so for a fiddly curve round up a couple of rolls.

    Before you order

    • Turf is cut to order and should be laid the day it arrives. Rolled up in a stack, it yellows within 48 hours.
    • Order for a Friday delivery and you'll have the weekend to lay it.
    • Lay it in staggered rows like brickwork, working off planks so you don't dent the soil.
    • Water every day for the first two weeks unless it rains properly.
