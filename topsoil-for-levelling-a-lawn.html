How much topsoil to level a lawn?

Short answer: Top-dressing a 50 m² lawn at 1–2 cm takes 550–1100 litres, or about 44 bags of 25 L at 2 cm.

    Don't bury the grass

    Never put more than about 2 cm on at once, or the grass underneath dies. For dips deeper than that, cut an H in the turf, fold the flaps back, fill underneath with soil, then fold them back down. Or level it in two or three goes a few weeks apart.

    The best time is early autumn or spring, after scarifying and aerating. Brush the dressing into the grass with the back of a rake so the blades still show.

    Questions people ask

    What's the difference between topsoil and lawn dressing?

    Lawn dressing is a sandier, finer mix that's easier to brush in. Screened topsoil works too.

    When should I top-dress a lawn?

    September is ideal. Spring works too.

    Before you order

    • Ask whether it's screened (sieved). Unscreened topsoil is cheaper but full of stones and roots.
    • A 0.75 m³ bulk bag weighs close to a tonne. You can't drag it; it has to be emptied where it lands.
    • Wet soil is heavy and smears. If you can, order for a dry spell.
    • For a raised bed, mix in about a third compost rather than using topsoil on its own.
