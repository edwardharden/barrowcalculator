    When to stop buying bags

    Once you need more than about 20 bags (half a cubic metre), a bulk bag is usually cheaper and saves a lot of trips. Bagged soil is handy for pots, small beds and gardens with no front access.

    Litres and cubic metres

    1 m³ = 1,000 litres. So 40 bags of 25 L is exactly one cubic metre.

    Questions people ask

    How many 25 L bags make a cubic metre?

    Forty.

    How much does a 25 L bag of topsoil cover?

    About 0.25 m² at 10 cm deep, or 1.25 m² at 2 cm.

    Before you order

    • Ask whether it's screened (sieved). Unscreened topsoil is cheaper but full of stones and roots.
    • A 0.75 m³ bulk bag weighs close to a tonne. You can't drag it; it has to be emptied where it lands.
    • Wet soil is heavy and smears. If you can, order for a dry spell.
    • For a raised bed, mix in about a third compost rather than using topsoil on its own.
