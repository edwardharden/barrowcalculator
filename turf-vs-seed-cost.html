Turf or seed: which is cheaper?

Short answer: Seed is far cheaper. A 40 m² lawn costs about £8 in seed against around £190 in turf. Turf gives you a usable lawn in weeks instead of months.

    When turf is worth it

    • You need the lawn usable in a few weeks (kids, dogs, selling the house).
    • It's late spring or summer, when seed struggles without lots of watering.
    • It's a slope, where seed washes off.

    When seed is worth it

    • Large areas, where the cost difference is huge.
    • Shady spots, where you can pick a seed mix suited to shade.
    • Early autumn sowing, when it practically looks after itself.

    Questions people ask

    How long before I can walk on new turf?

    Light use after 2–3 weeks once it's rooted. Seed takes 2–3 months.

    Is turf better quality than seed?

    Not necessarily. A well-sown lawn from a good seed mix is every bit as good after a year.

    Before you order

    • Turf is cut to order and should be laid the day it arrives. Rolled up in a stack, it yellows within 48 hours.
    • Order for a Friday delivery and you'll have the weekend to lay it.
    • Lay it in staggered rows like brickwork, working off planks so you don't dent the soil.
    • Water every day for the first two weeks unless it rains properly.
