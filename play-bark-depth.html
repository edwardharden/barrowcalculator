    Why so deep?

    Play bark only cushions a fall if there's enough of it. Around 30 cm is the depth commonly recommended under typical garden play equipment. Thinner layers get kicked away from under swings within weeks. Check the bag's certification (it should be tested to BS EN 1177) and the fall height it's rated for.

    Keep it topped up

    Rake bark back under swings and slides regularly, and top it up every year or two. Ordinary ornamental bark isn't a substitute. It isn't tested for impact and often contains splinters.

    Questions people ask

    How far should play bark extend around a swing?

    Well beyond the swing's full arc in both directions. Most manufacturers give a safety zone in the instructions.

    Do I need a membrane under play bark?

    Yes. It stops the bark mixing into the soil and keeps weeds down.

    Before you order

    • Water the soil and pull the weeds before you mulch. Bark holds in whatever's already there.
    • Keep bark a hand's width back from shrub stems and tree trunks.
    • Bark is light, so a bulk bag can sometimes go through a side gate if the driver will tip it.
    • Under swings and slides, use certified play bark at 30 cm. Ordinary ornamental bark isn't tested for falls.
