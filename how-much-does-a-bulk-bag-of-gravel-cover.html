    Before it arrives

    Bulk bags are about 90 cm square and arrive on a crane lorry, so the driver needs to park close by and swing it onto your drive or front garden. They won't go through a side gate. If the bag has to go on the pavement or road, check with your council first; some need a permit.

    Moving it: an 825 kg bag is about 7–8 wheelbarrow loads. Lay a board over the lawn if you're barrowing across it.

    Questions people ask

    How heavy is a bulk bag of gravel?

    Usually 800–900 kg. Some suppliers sell 'jumbo' bags closer to a tonne.

    Is a bulk bag cheaper than small bags?

    Usually much cheaper. 825 kg in 20 kg bags is 42 bags.

    Before you order

    • A bulk bag is roughly 90 cm square. It's dropped at the kerb by a crane lorry, so you need somewhere to put it, and it won't go through a side gate.
    • One bulk bag is about 7 or 8 wheelbarrow loads. Budget an hour per bag if you're barrowing it to the back garden.
    • Lay a permeable membrane first. Gravel straight onto soil sinks and grows weeds within a season.
    • Angular gravel (crushed stone) locks together and stays put on a drive. Rounded pea shingle rolls about and gets kicked onto the lawn.
