How much grass seed for overseeding?

Short answer: Overseed at about 25 g per m². A 50 m² lawn needs 1.25 kg of seed.

    Prepare first or don't bother

    Seed thrown onto an untouched lawn mostly feeds the birds. Mow short, scarify hard to pull out moss and thatch, and spike the lawn with a fork or aerator. Then sow, and brush in a thin layer of lawn dressing so the seed is in contact with soil.

    Keep it damp for the first two to three weeks. Early September is the best time, because the soil is still warm and the autumn rain does the watering.

    Questions people ask

    Can I overseed without scarifying?

    You can, but far less seed will germinate.

    How soon can I mow after overseeding?

    When the new grass reaches about 5–7 cm. Take only the top third off, with sharp blades.

    Before you order

    • Split the seed in two and sow half walking up and down, half side to side. It avoids stripes.
    • Rake it in lightly and roll or tread it. Seed that sits on the surface gets eaten by birds.
    • Don't use weed-and-feed for at least 6 months after sowing.
    • Hard-wearing mixes (with ryegrass) suit families and dogs. Fine mixes look better but sulk under football.
