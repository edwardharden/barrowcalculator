    Gravel depth guide

    Getting the depth right matters more than anything else. Too thin and the membrane shows through; too thick and gravel becomes hard to walk on and drive through.

    UseDepthGravel size
    Decorative topping / pots2–3 cm10–20 mm
    Paths and borders4–5 cm10–20 mm
    Driveways5 cm on a compacted sub-base14–20 mm

    How we calculate it

    Area × depth gives the volume in cubic metres. Gravel weighs roughly 1.6 tonnes per cubic metre, so we multiply by 1,600 kg. We add 10% by default, because gravel settles and spreads.

    Bags or bulk bags?

    For anything over about 0.5 tonnes, a bulk bag is usually far cheaper per kilo than 20kg bags, and it's delivered to your kerb. Small bags make sense for pots, small borders or where there's no access for delivery.

    Questions people ask

    How much area does a tonne of gravel cover?

    At 5 cm deep, one tonne covers roughly 12 m². At 4 cm it covers about 15 m².

    How many 20kg bags are in a tonne?

    Fifty 20kg bags make one tonne.

    Do I need a weed membrane?

    Yes, for paths and borders. A permeable membrane under the gravel stops weeds and keeps the gravel from sinking into the soil.

    Before you order

    • A bulk bag is roughly 90 cm square. It's dropped at the kerb by a crane lorry, so you need somewhere to put it, and it won't go through a side gate.
    • One bulk bag is about 7 or 8 wheelbarrow loads. Budget an hour per bag if you're barrowing it to the back garden.
    • Lay a permeable membrane first. Gravel straight onto soil sinks and grows weeds within a season.
    • Angular gravel (crushed stone) locks together and stays put on a drive. Rounded pea shingle rolls about and gets kicked onto the lawn.
