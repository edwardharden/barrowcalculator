    Gravel under decks

    The gravel isn't for show. Its job is to hold the weed membrane down and stop light getting to the soil. 4–5 cm is plenty.

    Cheapest way to do it

    Any cheap 10–20 mm gravel will do, because no one will see it. Lay a heavy-duty permeable membrane first, overlapping the joins by at least 15 cm, then spread the gravel before the joists go on if you can. It's much easier than working between them.

    Questions people ask

    Do I need gravel under decking?

    Not strictly, but a membrane on its own blows about and degrades in light. Gravel weighs it down.

    What size gravel under decking?

    10–20 mm. It doesn't need to be decorative.

    Before you order

    • A bulk bag is roughly 90 cm square. It's dropped at the kerb by a crane lorry, so you need somewhere to put it, and it won't go through a side gate.
    • One bulk bag is about 7 or 8 wheelbarrow loads. Budget an hour per bag if you're barrowing it to the back garden.
    • Lay a permeable membrane first. Gravel straight onto soil sinks and grows weeds within a season.
    • Angular gravel (crushed stone) locks together and stays put on a drive. Rounded pea shingle rolls about and gets kicked onto the lawn.
