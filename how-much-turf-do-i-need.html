How much turf do I need?

Short answer: Standard UK turf rolls are 1 m². For a 5 × 8 m lawn, order 42 rolls: the area plus 5% for trimming.

    Order for the day you'll lay it

    Turf is cut to order and starts to yellow within about 48 hours of being rolled up. Have the ground prepared before it arrives and lay it the same day. If you can't, unroll it flat somewhere shady and water it.

    Laying

    Start along a straight edge. Stagger the joints like brickwork and butt each roll tight against the last. Work from planks laid on the turf you've already put down, never on the prepared soil. Water heavily straight away, then daily for two weeks unless it rains properly.

    Questions people ask

    What size is a roll of turf?

    In the UK, almost always 1 m² (about 1.63 m × 0.61 m).

    How much extra turf should I order?

    5% for a simple rectangle, and up to 10% for curved or awkward shapes.

    Before you order

    • Turf is cut to order and should be laid the day it arrives. Rolled up in a stack, it yellows within 48 hours.
    • Order for a Friday delivery and you'll have the weekend to lay it.
    • Lay it in staggered rows like brickwork, working off planks so you don't dent the soil.
    • Water every day for the first two weeks unless it rains properly.
