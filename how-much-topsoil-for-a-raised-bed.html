How much soil for a raised bed?

Short answer: A 2.4 × 1.2 m raised bed, 30 cm deep, holds about 864 litres. That's 39 bags of 25 L, or about one bulk bag.

    What to fill it with

    For vegetables, mix roughly two parts topsoil to one part peat-free compost or well-rotted manure. Topsoil alone can be heavy and slow to drain. Compost alone shrinks and dries out quickly.

    For deep beds (over 40 cm), you can fill the bottom third with turf turned upside down, rotted wood or garden prunings to save on soil. It'll sink a bit over the first year, so top it up.

    Questions people ask

    How many bags of soil for a 6 × 3 ft raised bed?

    A 1.8 × 0.9 m bed at 30 cm needs about 22 bags of 25 L.

    Should I use topsoil or compost in a raised bed?

    A mix. Around two-thirds topsoil to one-third compost.

    Before you order

    • Ask whether it's screened (sieved). Unscreened topsoil is cheaper but full of stones and roots.
    • A 0.75 m³ bulk bag weighs close to a tonne. You can't drag it; it has to be emptied where it lands.
    • Wet soil is heavy and smears. If you can, order for a dry spell.
    • For a raised bed, mix in about a third compost rather than using topsoil on its own.
