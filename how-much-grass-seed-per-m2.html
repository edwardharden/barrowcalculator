How much grass seed per m²?

Short answer: Sow 50 g per m² for a new lawn, 25 g per m² to overseed an existing one, and about 35 g per m² for patch repairs.

    Check the box

    Most UK lawn seed is sown at 35–50 g/m² for new lawns. Some coated seeds ask for less because the coating bulks them out. The rate printed on the box wins over any general guide.

    Sow it evenly

    Weigh 50 g into a cup so you can see what it looks like on a square metre. Split the seed in half and sow one half walking up and down, and the other half side to side.

    Questions people ask

    Can I sow too much grass seed?

    Yes. Overcrowded seedlings compete and are more prone to disease. Stick to the rate.

    When is the best time to sow grass seed in the UK?

    Late August to late September is best. Mid-April to May is second best.

    Before you order

    • Split the seed in two and sow half walking up and down, half side to side. It avoids stripes.
    • Rake it in lightly and roll or tread it. Seed that sits on the surface gets eaten by birds.
    • Don't use weed-and-feed for at least 6 months after sowing.
    • Hard-wearing mixes (with ryegrass) suit families and dogs. Fine mixes look better but sulk under football.
