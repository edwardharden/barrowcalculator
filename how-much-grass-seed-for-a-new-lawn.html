How much grass seed for a new lawn?

Short answer: A new lawn needs about 50 g of seed per m². A typical 5 × 10 m garden lawn needs 2.5 kg.

    Choosing a seed

    For a family lawn that gets football and dogs, pick a hard-wearing mix with perennial ryegrass. For a fine ornamental lawn, use a mix of fescues and bents. These look better close-mown but don't stand up to wear. Shady lawns need a shade mix.

    Soil first

    Dig or rotavate, remove stones and roots, rake level, tread it firm, and rake again. Sow on a still day and rake lightly so the seed is just covered.

    Questions people ask

    How long does grass seed take to grow?

    Usually 7–14 days to germinate in warm, damp soil. A usable lawn takes 8–12 weeks.

    Should I put topsoil down before seeding?

    If your soil is poor, yes. Spread 5–10 cm of screened topsoil.

    Before you order

    • Split the seed in two and sow half walking up and down, half side to side. It avoids stripes.
    • Rake it in lightly and roll or tread it. Seed that sits on the surface gets eaten by birds.
    • Don't use weed-and-feed for at least 6 months after sowing.
    • Hard-wearing mixes (with ryegrass) suit families and dogs. Fine mixes look better but sulk under football.
