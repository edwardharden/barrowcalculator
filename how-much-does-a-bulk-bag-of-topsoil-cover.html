How much does a bulk bag of topsoil cover?

Short answer: A 0.75 m³ bulk bag of topsoil covers about 6.8 m² at 10 cm deep, or 34 m² at 2 cm, allowing 10% for settling.

    Weight

    Topsoil weighs about 1.3 tonnes per m³ when dry, and more when wet. A 0.75 m³ bag can be close to a tonne. It has to be emptied where it lands.

    Questions people ask

    How many wheelbarrows in a bulk bag of topsoil?

    A 0.75 m³ bag is roughly 10–11 barrow loads.

    Can a bulk bag go through a side gate?

    No. It arrives on a crane lorry and is dropped at the front.

    Before you order

    • Ask whether it's screened (sieved). Unscreened topsoil is cheaper but full of stones and roots.
    • A 0.75 m³ bulk bag weighs close to a tonne. You can't drag it; it has to be emptied where it lands.
    • Wet soil is heavy and smears. If you can, order for a dry spell.
    • For a raised bed, mix in about a third compost rather than using topsoil on its own.
