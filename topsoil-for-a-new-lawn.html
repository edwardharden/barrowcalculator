Short answer: For a 5 × 8 m lawn with 10 cm of topsoil, order about 4.4 m³. That's 6 bulk bags of 0.75 m³.

    Topsoil for lawns

    New turf or seed needs 10–15 cm of good topsoil for roots to grow into. If your existing soil is decent, you only need a few centimetres to level it.

    What to ask for

    Ask for screened topsoil, which has been sieved to remove stones and roots. It's worth the extra money for a lawn. Spread it, rake it level, firm it by treading it in, then rake again. Leave it a week to settle if you can.

    Questions people ask

    How deep should topsoil be for turf?

    10–15 cm.

    Can I lay turf on clay?

    Yes, but add at least 10 cm of topsoil on top and dig in some sharp sand first.

    Before you order

    • Ask whether it's screened (sieved). Unscreened topsoil is cheaper but full of stones and roots.
    • A 0.75 m³ bulk bag weighs close to a tonne. You can't drag it; it has to be emptied where it lands.
    • Wet soil is heavy and smears. If you can, order for a dry spell.
    • For a raised bed, mix in about a third compost rather than using topsoil on its own.
