    When to sow

    In the UK the best times are early autumn (September) and mid-spring (April–May), when the soil is warm and moist. Keep the seed watered until it's well established.

    Common questions

    How much grass seed per square metre?

    Around 50 g/m² for a new lawn and 25 g/m² for overseeding.

    How long does grass seed take to grow?

    Usually 7–21 days to germinate in good conditions, and about 8 weeks before light use.

    Is seed or turf better?

    Seed is much cheaper but slower. Turf gives an instant lawn but costs more and must be laid within a day or so of delivery.
