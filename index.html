Why use a calculator?

Over-ordering wastes money and leaves a bulk bag on your drive. Under-ordering means paying for a second delivery. Our calculators use standard UK densities and recommended depths, and add sensible extra for settling and trimming.

How to measure

Use a tape measure to get the length and width in metres. For awkward shapes, split the area into rectangles, work each one out, and add them together using "Known area".
