Measuring up

Use a tape, not a guess. Measure length and width in metres. For an L-shaped patio or a curved border, split it into rectangles, work out each area, add them up and use "I know the area".

Depth is where most people go wrong. 4 cm of gravel looks thin when you picture it, but it's right for a path. Go deeper and it's like walking on a beach.

Why we add 10%

Loose materials settle once they're raked and walked on, and some always ends up on the drive. The extra is on by default; untick it if you'd rather cut it fine.

Bags or bulk bags?

  • Under about half a tonne of gravel, or for pots and small borders, small bags are easier.
  • Above that, a bulk bag is usually much cheaper per kilo, but it needs a kerbside drop.
  • Most bulk bags won't fit through a side gate. Plan on a lot of wheelbarrow trips.
