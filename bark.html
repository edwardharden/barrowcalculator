    How we calculate it

    Area × depth gives the volume. Bark is sold by volume rather than weight, so we show litres and cubic metres. We add 10% because bark settles over the first few weeks.

    When to mulch

    Spread bark in spring or autumn onto moist, weeded soil. Keep it a few centimetres away from plant stems and tree trunks to prevent rot.

    Common questions

    How much does a 70L bag of bark cover?

    At 5 cm deep, a 70-litre bag covers about 1.4 m².

    How often should I top up bark?

    Most bark mulch breaks down over two to three years, so top it up every year or two.

    Is play bark different?

    Yes. Play bark is tested for impact absorption and sharp splinters. Use it under play equipment.
