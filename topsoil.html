    How we calculate it

    Area × depth gives the volume in cubic metres (1 m³ = 1,000 litres). We add 10% by default, because loose topsoil compacts as it settles.

    Raised beds

    For a raised bed, measure the inside length and width and use the bed's full height as the depth. Many gardeners fill the bottom third with cheaper subsoil or garden waste.

    Common questions

    How much does a bulk bag of topsoil cover?

    A typical 0.75 m³ bulk bag covers about 7.5 m² at 10 cm deep.

    How many 25L bags are in a cubic metre?

    Forty 25-litre bags make one cubic metre.

    What's the difference between topsoil and compost?

    Topsoil is the structural base. Compost is organic matter you mix in to feed it. Most beds benefit from both.
