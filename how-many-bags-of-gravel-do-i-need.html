    When small bags make sense

    Up to about 25 bags, small bags are fine. You can carry them through the house, store them in a shed and spread them over a weekend. Beyond that, a bulk bag is almost always cheaper per kilo, but it gets dropped at the kerb and has to be barrowed in.

    Working it out yourself

    Multiply length × width × depth (in metres) to get cubic metres, then multiply by 1.6 to get tonnes. Divide the kilos by 20 for the number of 20 kg bags. Round up, always.

    Questions people ask

    How many 20 kg bags of gravel are in a tonne?

    Fifty.

    How many bags of gravel for 1 m²?

    At 4 cm deep, about 70 kg, which is four 20 kg bags with the 10% extra included.

    Is a bulk bag the same as a tonne?

    Not quite. Most gravel bulk bags hold 800–900 kg. Check the listing.

    Before you order

    • A bulk bag is roughly 90 cm square. It's dropped at the kerb by a crane lorry, so you need somewhere to put it, and it won't go through a side gate.
    • One bulk bag is about 7 or 8 wheelbarrow loads. Budget an hour per bag if you're barrowing it to the back garden.
    • Lay a permeable membrane first. Gravel straight onto soil sinks and grows weeds within a season.
    • Angular gravel (crushed stone) locks together and stays put on a drive. Rounded pea shingle rolls about and gets kicked onto the lawn.
