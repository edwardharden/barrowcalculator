    Ordering turf

    Most UK turf is sold in 1 m² rolls. We add 5% for trimming around curves and edges. For very irregular shapes, add 10%.

    Prepare first

    Dig or rotavate, remove stones and roots, level with about 10 cm of good topsoil, then firm and rake. Lay the turf within 24 hours of delivery, staggered like brickwork, and water thoroughly.

    Best time to lay

    Autumn and spring are ideal. You can lay turf in summer if you can water it every day.

    Common questions

    How big is a roll of turf?

    Usually 1 m², often about 1.6 m × 0.6 m. Check your supplier.

    How long before I can walk on new turf?

    Keep off for about two to three weeks, until the roots have taken hold.

    Do I need topsoil under turf?

    Yes. 10–15 cm of good topsoil gives the roots something to grow into. Use our topsoil calculator.
